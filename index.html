Can I save this to my phone home screen?

Yes. Once hosted, use Share > Add to Home Screen on iPhone or Add to Home screen from Chrome on Android.

Will the site need constant updates?

No. The draft is structured around evergreen guides, with optional spots for rankings and current reminders.
