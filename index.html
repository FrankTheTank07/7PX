Alliance Rules

  • Always read announcements when you log in. We do not post many each day, so a minute or less of reading will usually get you caught up.
  • If leadership reaches out, please respond in a timely fashion. Keep notifications on so you see in-game messages and @ mentions.
  • We joke around and like to have fun, but do not go overly offensive. Read the room.
  • Weekly VS goals are 7.2M points per day and 43.2M points per week.
  • Join events when possible. We win together as a team, and the more members who participate, the better chance we have of succeeding.
  • During alliance events like Marshal's Guard or Zombie Siege, please refrain from launching digs or starting rallies.
  • Help keep the hive neat and orderly. Please line up with no spaces or gaps between HQs unless told otherwise.

Events

Alliance Event

Marshal's Guard

  • On days this event is available, it starts 15 minutes after reset.
  • Create rallies with your weakest squad.
  • Join other rallies with your stronger squads.

Alliance Event

Zombie Siege

  • On days this event is available, it starts 15 minutes after reset.
  • Make sure your troops and squads are assigned to your wall before the event starts.
  • Zombie Siege has 20 waves. Every four waves sends out buses of zombies.
  • Help defend against the zombie buses to earn extra rewards.
