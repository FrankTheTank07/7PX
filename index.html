Alliance Rules

  • Always read announcements when you log in. We do not post many each day, so a minute or less of reading will usually get you caught up.
  • If leadership reaches out, please respond in a timely fashion. Keep notifications on so you see in-game messages and @ mentions.
  • We joke around and like to have fun, but do not go overly offensive. Read the room.
  • Weekly VS goals are 7.2M points per day and 43.2M points per week.
  • Join events when possible. We win together as a team, and the more members who participate, the better chance we have of succeeding.
  • During alliance events like Marshal's Guard or Zombie Siege, please refrain from launching digs or starting rallies.
  • Help keep the hive neat and orderly. Please line up with no spaces or gaps between HQs unless told otherwise.
  • As a courtesy, if you join the nightly train for rewards, please donate the maximum of three tickets. This helps replenish the conductor's ticket stash after they refresh the train for better rewards.

Alliance Event

Marshal's Guard

  • On days this event is available, it starts 15 minutes after reset.
  • Create rallies with your weakest squad.
  • Join other rallies with your stronger squads.
  • Periodically, bulls will be sent out from the boss. Attacking and defeating those bulls will significantly increase your points and help defeat the boss.
  • You can earn great rewards for hitting your individual goal and for participating in general.

Alliance Event

Zombie Siege

  • On days this event is available, it starts 15 minutes after reset.
  • Make sure your troops and squads are assigned to your wall before the event starts.
  • Zombie Siege has 20 waves. Every four waves sends out buses of zombies.
  • Help defend against the zombie buses to earn extra rewards.

Alliance Event

Desert Storm

  • Desert Storm is a team battle where alliances fight to control multiple structures across the map.
  • Each structure can award a different amount of points per second. The team with the most points at the end wins.
  • We can have 30 total participants: 20 active players and 10 substitutes.
  • Registration is first come, first served, so keep an eye on announcements to see when signups open.
  • The rewards are strong whether we win or lose, but winning gives the best payout.
  • Troops lost during Desert Storm are fully restored when the event ends. You do not permanently lose troops by participating.
  • You can still lose resources if you spend resources to speed up healing during the event.
  • Team A runs at 23:00 ST. Team B runs at 18:00 ST. Depending on your time zone and location, this may fall on Friday or Saturday.

Alliance Event

Canyon Storm

  • Canyon Storm is similar to Desert Storm: alliances fight to control multiple structures across the map.
  • Each structure can award a different amount of points per second. The team with the most points at the end wins.
  • The big difference is that Canyon Storm is a 2v1 event. Depending on the faction, two alliances may fight against one alliance.
  • We can have 30 total participants: 20 active players and 10 substitutes.
  • Registration is first come, first served, so keep an eye on announcements to see when signups open.
  • The rewards are strong whether we win or lose, but winning gives the best payout.
  • Troops lost during Canyon Storm are fully restored when the event ends. You do not permanently lose troops by participating.
  • You can still lose resources if you spend resources to speed up healing during the event.
  • Team A runs at 23:00 ST. Team B runs at 12:00 ST. Depending on your time zone and location, this may fall on Thursday or Friday.
